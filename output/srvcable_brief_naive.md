# Equity Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE)

---

### **1. Snapshot**
* **Business Overview:** Manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors catering to power utilities, industrial infrastructure, data centres, and export markets.
* **Current Share Price:** ₹1,012 *(as of post-Q1 FY27 results, down 3.2%)* [S1]
* **Financial Performance (Q1 FY27):**
  * **Revenue from Operations:** ₹1,248 crore, +18.0% YoY [S3] *(Note: Media report [S1] cited ₹1,428 cr / +35%, but official company filings confirm ₹1,248 cr [S3])*.
  * **EBITDA / Margin:** ₹140 crore (+2.6% YoY); EBITDA margin contracted 170 bps YoY to 11.2% due to a ~14% increase in copper prices [S1, S3].
  * **Profit After Tax (PAT):** ₹82 crore (+5.9% YoY) [S3].
* **Balance Sheet & Leverage:** Net debt-to-equity is comfortable at 0.35x [S2].
* **Order Book & Growth Guidance:** Order book at ₹3,900 crore (+23.8% YoY from ₹3,150 crore) [S3]. Management guidance for FY27 revenue growth is 15–17% [S2].
* **Shareholding & Governance:** Promoters hold 58.2%. Pledged shares have fallen substantially to **4.1%** of promoter holding (June 2026) [S7], down from 35% in March 2024 [S4]. Combined institutional holding (FII + DII) stands at 26.4% [S7].

---

### **2. Bull Case**
* **Upcoming Capacity Expansion:** The ₹620 crore Bharuch (Gujarat) plant is ~70% funded/completed and scheduled for commercial production in Q3 FY27, expanding total cable capacity by ~30% [S2, S3].
* **High-Growth Segment Mix:** 
  * Data-centre cables account for ~9% of domestic revenue, with management projecting this segment to double over the next two years [S2].
  * Export mix expanded to 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27 (up from 16% in Q1 FY26) [S3].
* **Contractual Margin Protection:** Roughly two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, allowing raw material cost increases (copper) to be passed through with a 1-to-2 quarter lag [S2, S3]. Management noted no irrational competitor pricing [S2].
* **Balance Sheet Health & De-pledging:** Net debt remains conservative (0.35x D/E) despite ongoing capex [S2]. Significant reduction in promoter share pledging from 35% (March 2024) to 4.1% (June 2026) removes a major governance overhang [S4, S7].

---

### **3. Bear Case**
* **Working Capital Deterioration:** Working capital cycle is lengthening; receivable days increased from 78 to 96 days over the trailing year, driven by payment delays from two large state utility customers [S2].
* **Raw Material Volatility & Pass-Through Lag:** Copper price spikes (~14% in Q1) directly squeezed operating margins by 170 bps [S1, S3]. Approximately one-third of the order portfolio lacks price-variation clauses, exposing margins to fixed-price commodity risk [S2].
* **Contingent Tax Liability:** In September 2026, the company received a ₹46.3 crore GST demand order (tax, interest, and penalty) from CGST Mumbai regarding historical ITC claims—a figure representing more than 56% of Q1 FY27 net profit [S6].
* **Execution Risk:** Achieving full-year revenue growth guidance of 15–17% relies heavily on timely operational commissioning of the Bharuch plant in H2 FY27 [S2].

---

### **4. Open Questions for Management**
1. **Receivables Aging:** What is the recovery timeline for outstanding dues from the two state utility clients, and is there any risk of provisioning if delays extend past 120 days?
2. **Margin Trough:** Will the lag in price-variation resets compress Q2 margins further before recovery in H2 FY27?
3. **Litigation Strategy:** What is the legal basis and timeline for the appeal against the ₹46.3 crore CGST demand, and will any pre-deposit be required to stay the order?
4. **Bharuch Utilization:** What is the expected capacity utilization and margin profile of the Bharuch plant in its first 12 months of operations?
