# SRVCABLE: research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. In Q1 FY27, revenue from operations grew 18.0% year-on-year to ₹1,248 crore, while profit after tax (PAT) rose to ₹82 crore [S3]. The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026 [S3].

## Bull case
- **Healthy demand and growing order backlog:** The order book expanded from ₹3,150 crore to ₹3,900 crore year-on-year [S3], providing revenue visibility as management maintains full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15% to 17% [S2].
- **Upcoming capacity expansion:** The ₹620 crore Bharuch plant (about 70% already spent) will add about 30% to cable capacity and is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S2, S3].
- **Diversification into exports and data centres:** Exports rose to 21% of revenue from 16% a year ago [S3], and data-centre cables account for about 9% of domestic revenue, with management estimating that share can double in two years [S2].
- **Decline in promoter share pledging:** Pledged shares (shares promoters have put up as loan collateral) fell to 4.1% of promoter holdings in June 2026 from 11.5% in June 2025 [S7], sharply lowering lender-liquidation risk compared to historical levels.

## Bear case
- **Raw material margin squeeze:** EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points year-on-year to 11.2% in Q1 FY27 due to an approximate 14% rise in copper prices [S1, S3], while price-variation clauses cover only about two-thirds of contracts and reset with a lag of one to two quarters [S2].
- **Deteriorating working capital:** Receivable days rose from 78 to 96 days over the past year, mainly driven by slower payments from two large state utility customers [S2], tying up cash.
- **Material GST tax dispute:** The company received a ₹46.3 crore GST demand order including interest and penalty [S6]. This represents 56.5% of Q1 FY27 profit after tax (₹82 crore) [S3, S6], creating a substantial contingent cash risk if the appeal fails.

## Open questions
- **Revenue figure mismatch:** News source [S1] reported Q1 revenue of ₹1,428 crore (+35%), whereas the company's official financial release [S3] states ₹1,248 crore (+18.0%). We prioritize the company filing [S3].
- **Reported growth arithmetic:** In [S3], reported EBITDA growth of 2.6% (₹136 crore to ₹140 crore) recomputes to 2.9%. Reported PAT growth of 5.9% (₹77 crore to ₹82 crore) recomputes to 6.5%.
- **GST order impact:** Management asserts it does not expect any material impact and plans an appeal [S6], but the pack lacks filing updates, pre-deposit status, or provision details for this ₹46.3 crore demand.
- **Missing current market price:** The stock price is not reported as of today (2026-09-23); the only price in the pack is ₹1,012 from 2026-08-08 [S1].
